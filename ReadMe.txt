Controls:

WASD Move

Left Click To Shoot Current Gun

Mouse wheel to change current gun

Shift + Mouse wheel to change current resource

Q to Throw Resource

F to punch/collect crops

Water and sun (verb) the wheat after planting it to grow it, punch when fully grown to get the wheat, throw wheat at the bread oven, and then take the bread and throw it at the bread stand to sell it.  At night go to the bread stand to collect your money, and then use it to buy upgrades all over the map.  Simply walking into the little colliders will spend money on the upgrade.

Bread Oven: You need to keep the bread oven hot enough to work, but not too hot to burn the bread.  If oven is too cold then baking will be very slow.  Shoot with sun to heat up, shoot with water to cool down.  Based on the color of the text you'll know if it's too hot/cold.

Advancing to next area:  There's a few areas blocked by huge trees that need tons of water/sun.  Get upgrades to be able to destroy it.

Storm clouds: Once unlocking them storm clouds will appear in sky rarely that you can shoot to start a storm cloud of that same resource (i.e. shooting with water causes rain, sun causes sun storm) that automatically applies that resource to all plants in the map

SunFlowers/WaterMelon: Crops that are unlocked with second area.  Not sure if they're actually useful or annoying yet.  (end of part 1)

These crops are grown like any other, but once harvested you can throw them back at the ground to explode in sun/water respectively and quickly grow crops.  Again, they might not be worth the resources you need to put into them, so not sure about it yet.

Bread Pricing:  Each day you have to sell a certain amount of bread to multiply the amount of money you made by a multiplier.  Each day you meet your quota, the multiplier goes up.  If you miss a quota, it goes back down to 0.  Each morning you can accept/reject a perk that makes your character better, but will increase the amount you'll need to bake.

Wind: In the third area it's affected by wind and so you will be pushed in random directions the entire time.  Be aware.

Love Gun:  Hold left click to charge a shot, and once a plant is "loved" it will drop it's own seed after being destroyed

Grenade Launcher:  Can destroy crops and also launch you around the map to new areas

Tab: Press tab to see plant health bars and also to be able to see a few of your character's stats.  Will need to add many more.

Cheats: Press ~ and all the cheats can be searched literally by typing in "Brock".  If you want to spawn in resources with the cheat just put  "ResourceType." and then the resource that you want (bread, wheat, money, etc.). So to give yourself 500 money do AddResourcesBrock ResourceType.Money 500
